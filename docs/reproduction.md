```sh
.venv/bin/python scripts/download_data.py --output-dir data/reproduction-check
.venv/bin/python scripts/profile_dataset.py --output-dir data/reproduction-check
.venv/bin/python scripts/supplemental_analysis.py --output-dir data/reproduction-check
.venv/bin/python scripts/repository_counts.py --output-dir data/reproduction-check
```

Compare the six generated files with their equivalents in `results/202610/`: `download-manifest.json`, `profile.json`, `supplemental-profile.json`, `repeated-pair-keys.json`, `repository-counts.csv`, and `repository-counts-summary.json`.
